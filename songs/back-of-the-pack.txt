Boom bap. The funny one. About the half of the club that finishes last
and has the best time doing it.

NOTES
================================================================

- Keep it warm. The joke is affectionate: the back of the pack is where the
  club actually lives. If a take comes back sounding tough or boastful, that
  is the wrong take - regenerate rather than keep it.

- [Breakdown] must stay sparse. Adding "(drums out, spoken)" next to the tag
  usually stops Suno turning it into a fourth chorus.

- The crowd shouts in brackets are the point of the hook. If Suno sings them
  as normal lines, try putting them on their own line in capitals.

- "Prepa" is written without the accent on purpose - Suno mispronounces
  "Prepa" less often this way. Listen for it; if it sounds wrong, try
  "Pre-pa" with the hyphen.

- Generate 4-6 takes and judge them on verse 2 and the breakdown. The hook
  will be fine on almost every take; the storytelling is where they differ.

- Names to listen for: Shaghig, Asdghig, Jebid. Suno usually gets them close.
  If a take mangles them badly it is worth regenerating for that alone.
