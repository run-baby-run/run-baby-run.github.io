Late-night house. Written for the Tuesday 22:00 run.

Suno has two boxes. Paste the first block into Style, the second into Lyrics.

NOTES
================================================================

- The club name lands 32 times. That is the point of the track: anyone who
  hears it once should know what the club is called.

- Keep [Breakdown] sparse. If Suno fills it in, it becomes a fourth chorus and
  the last drop stops meaning anything. Adding "(sparse, drums out)" next to
  the tag usually fixes it.

- Generate 4-6 takes. House lives or dies on whether the drop actually lands,
  and Suno is inconsistent there. Judge takes on the first drop and the
  breakdown; the verses will be fine either way.

- If a take is good but the intro drags, regenerate with "no long intro" added
  to the style box.
